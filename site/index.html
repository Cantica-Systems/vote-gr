    Why this exists, and where the data comes from

    Your ward, precinct and voting location come from the state's voter file, and the state's voter site is the only official way to see them. It requires you to identify yourself in order to receive them, asking your name, birth month and year, and registration ZIP, and the privacy notice you must accept says the department "is subject to the Michigan Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) ... and information such as a name or address may be disclosed in response to a FOIA request". That notice does not exempt the lookup, so the details you enter and the fact you looked up your voting information could be subject to FOIA. It also answers about you, not about an address. That is the gap this fills, by comparing an address against the same published boundaries.

    The address you type never leaves your browser. There is no geocoder and no lookup service: the page downloads a list of city addresses and the precinct each one votes in, and the search runs against that list on your own device. Nobody receives what you typed, including us. This page, votegr.cantica.dev, is a live demo, hosted by Cloudflare, which serves the files and keeps its own request logs as any host does. That is not under our control, and if you would rather it did not apply, the files are yours to take, host, and redistribute.

    Precinct boundaries come from the State of Michigan. Polling places come from the City Clerk's precinct directory. Both are public records, passed along as published, and the clerk's directory is checked against Kent County's listing.

    Unofficial tool. Not run by the city, county, or state, and offered with no guarantee of accuracy. The Michigan Voter Information Center is the official source. Always verify there, or with the Grand Rapids City Clerk, before you rely on this.
